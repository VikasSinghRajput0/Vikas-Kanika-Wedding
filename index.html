Devbhoomi Heritage

🏔️ Himachali Culture & Traditions

Experience the timeless warmth of Himachal Pradesh — from the royal traditional Dhaam feast to Pahadi Nati folk melodies.

The Royal Himachali Dhaam

The legendary multi-course Satvik festive feast cooked in brass vessels (Charotis) by master Botis — featuring Sepu Badi, Madra, Khatta, and delicious Mittha served on leaf Pattals.

Pahadi Nati & Folk Songs

A lively atmosphere filled with folk wedding songs (Suhag & Ghorian), Karnal, Dhol, and traditional Himachali Nati dance celebrating love and brotherhood.

Devbhoomi Vedic Vivah

Seven sacred vows taken in the lap of the pristine Shivalik hills, invoking the blessings of Kuldevtas, Kuldevis, and ancestral deities for perpetual prosperity.

Festive Gastronomy • शाही दावत

The Royal Himachali Dhaam Menu

पारम्परिक बोटी (रसोइयों) द्वारा पीतल की चरोटियों में पकाई गई अमृतमयी सात्विक दावत
Sepu Badi / Chana Madra
चना एवं सेपू बड़ी मद्रा

The crown jewel of Himachali Dhaam! Lentil dumplings and chickpeas slow-simmered in rich spiced curd gravy with whole dry fruits, cardamom, and pure ghee.

Telia Mah (Maa ki Daal)
तेलिया माह (पारम्परिक दाल)

Whole black gram slow-cooked for hours in pure mustard oil, asafoetida, and aromatic hill spices for an unforgettable authentic Pahadi earthy aroma.

Himachali Khatta
शाही हिमाचली खट्टा

The signature palate-cleanser of Dhaam. A mouth-watering, perfectly balanced tangy gravy made of dried mango (amchur), tamarind, jaggery, and dry fruits.

Mahni & Moong Dal
मूंग दाल एवं महणी

Nourishing, wholesome golden lentils infused with cumin, ginger, and green chillies, prepared according to traditional Vedic satvik recipes.

Meetha Bhaat (Mittha)
मीठा भात / शाही ज़र्दा

The sweet finale! Fragrant Basmati rice infused with golden Kashmiri saffron, sweetened with jaggery/sugar, and garnished with plump raisins, almonds, and coconut.

Boondi Raita / Badana
बूंदी रायता एवं बदाना

Fresh creamy mountain curd blended with roasted cumin, rock salt, mint, and crisp boondi pearls, served fresh on natural eco-friendly Pattal leaves.
